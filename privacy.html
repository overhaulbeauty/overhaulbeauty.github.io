Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy covers the website overhaulbeauty.github.io and Overhaul Beauty Publisher, the internal tool Overhaul Beauty uses to publish its own content to its own TikTok, Instagram and Threads accounts.

Who uses the tool

Only the operator of Overhaul Beauty. The tool is not offered to the public and does not let other people log in.

What data the tool accesses

  • TikTok: the basic profile of the Overhaul Beauty account (open ID, display name, avatar) to confirm which account is connected, and permission to upload Overhaul Beauty's own videos to that account's TikTok inbox as drafts.
  • Instagram and Threads: the Overhaul Beauty account ID and username, permission to publish Overhaul Beauty's own videos, posts and replies, and the basic insights (views, likes, replies) of those posts.

The tool does not collect data about other users, followers or viewers, does not read messages, and does not use data for advertising.

Where the data is stored

Access tokens and post results are stored only on the operator's own computer. Published videos are hosted temporarily on this website (GitHub Pages) so the platforms can fetch them, and are deleted after 30 days. No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Website

This website has no accounts, no forms, no cookies and no tracking. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log technical data (such as IP addresses) as described in GitHub's own privacy statement.

Affiliate links

Our posts may contain affiliate links (for example Shopee). When you click them, the shop handles your visit under its own privacy policy.

Deleting data

See Data Deletion. You can also remove the tool's access at any time in your TikTok, Instagram or Threads settings.
