Data Deletion

Overhaul Beauty Publisher only stores data about Overhaul Beauty's own accounts (access tokens and the results of its own posts). It stores no data about other users.

How to request deletion

  1. Email overhaulbeauty@proton.me with the subject "Data deletion" and the account it is about.
  2. We delete the stored data within 7 days and confirm by email.

Remove access yourself

  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions → remove Overhaul Beauty Publisher.
  • Instagram: Settings → Website permissions / Apps and websites → remove Overhaul Beauty Publisher.
  • Threads: Settings → Account → Website permissions → remove Overhaul Beauty Publisher.
